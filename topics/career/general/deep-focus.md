# Deep focus

Meaningful output comes from protecting uninterrupted blocks of
concentrated work. The challenge is not working harder but defending
those blocks from fragmentation. Even two hours daily, if truly
unbroken, can drive most of a day's real progress.

## Consume with intent

Choose what to read and watch before opening a feed. Algorithms
optimize for engagement, not for your goals — letting them choose
your input means surrendering your attention to someone else's
priorities.

## Batch reactive tasks

Context-switching from emails, messages, and notifications destroys
focus. Group all reactive work (email, chat, meetings) into scheduled
blocks. Preserve unbroken stretches for head-down work.

## Designate a focus environment

Reserve a specific physical space for deep work only. Never browse,
scroll, or perform distracting habits in that space. Over time, the
consistent pairing of location and focused effort conditions the
brain to enter a concentrated state on arrival.
