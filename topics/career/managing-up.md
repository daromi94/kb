# Managing up

Moving from baseline performer to star does not happen through
gradual improvement in daily tasks. It happens in discrete leaps
when you take high-leverage actions that make your manager's life
easier.

## Act visibly on feedback

Do not just agree with constructive criticism. Ask for feedback
proactively, then demonstrate tangible change in your behavior or
output. Show growth, do not just talk about it.

## Provide exceptional status updates

Managers operate in an information desert and hate chasing updates.
Deliver proactive summaries before being asked: what you have
accomplished, current focus, blockers, and proposed solutions.

## Get ahead of bad news

Flag problems early. Present each issue alongside its potential
impact, resolution options, and your specific recommendation.
Managers despise surprises that make them feel out of control.

## Anticipate hidden needs

Move beyond request-response. Identify recurring friction points or
workflow bottlenecks on the team, build a solution without being
asked, and share it while keeping your manager in the loop.

## Align with your manager's goals

Your manager has a boss and their own metrics. Find out what
specific deliverables they are evaluated on and actively align your
work to help them succeed.
