# Effective one-on-ones

Most people waste their 1:1s by treating them as status reports.
Your relationship with your manager is the biggest predictor of
career success, so treat this dedicated time accordingly.

## The three conversations

Effective 1:1s maintain three distinct, ongoing threads:

| Conversation  | Focus                                          |
|---------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Status        | Current work, blockers, priorities             |
| Career growth | Development goals, manager support, trajectory |
| The future    | Strategic direction, org shifts, the "why"     |

Status discussions feel comfortable and urgent, so they crowd out the
other two if left unchecked.

## Move status updates async

Send a short, bulleted status update before the meeting. This
resolves quick questions over chat, creates a paper trail for
performance reviews, and frees face-to-face time for deeper
conversations.

## Keep career growth warm

Do not wait until you want a promotion to raise career topics — a
cold start feels awkward and leaves your manager without time to
position you. Ask small, specific questions regularly: what "good"
looks like at the next level, how to close skill gaps, where to
find stretch opportunities.

## Discuss the bigger picture

Ask your manager about company decisions you do not understand
instead of venting to peers. They have top-down context you lack;
you have ground-level insights (customer complaints, code friction)
they cannot see. Raising strategy or industry trends signals you are
thinking beyond your own backlog.
