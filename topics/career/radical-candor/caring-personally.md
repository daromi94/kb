# Caring personally

Care Personally does not mean personal affection. It means common
human decency and a commitment to someone's success. You do not need
to like a colleague to operate in the Radical Candor quadrant.

## What caring looks like without affinity

**Acknowledge their humanity.** Recognize they have goals, fears, and
a life outside of work. Basic respect satisfies the Care axis.

**Invest in their growth.** Care about the work they do and their
ability to succeed at it. Direct feedback is itself an act of care —
it prevents stagnation and failure.

**Check your own bias.** Disliking someone pulls you toward Obnoxious
Aggression (challenging to win) or Manipulative Insincerity (ignoring
them or talking behind their back). Awareness of the bias helps you
stay professional.

## How dislike distorts feedback

| Behavior                          | Quadrant                 | Result                                       |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Withhold feedback to avoid        | Manipulative Insincerity | Problems persist, silence reads as toxic     |
| Attack their character            | Obnoxious Aggression     | Defensiveness, you become the problem        |
| Give clear, work-focused feedback | Radical Candor           | Work improves, relationship stays functional |

## Practical rule

Keep feedback specific, sincere, and helpful. Focus on the impact of
behavior on the project or team, not on the person's character. Shift
from "you as a person" to "the result we need to achieve."
