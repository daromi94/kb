# Empathy vs. compassion

Empathy is a feeling. Compassion is empathy plus action. Ruinous
Empathy fails because it stops at the feeling — prioritizing
short-term comfort over long-term growth.

## Why empathy alone fails

**Emotional clouding.** Empathy is a "feeling with" response. Feeling
someone's pain too intensely clouds your reasoning. You focus on their
immediate distress and cannot make the rational decision to tell the
hard truth.

**Present-moment bias.** Empathy fixates on the now. Not telling
someone they are underperforming saves them pain today but leads to
them getting fired six months later.

## The boulder analogy

**Empathetic response:** You see someone crushed by a boulder, feel
the same suffocation, and become just as overwhelmed. You are helpless.

**Compassionate response:** You recognize their pain but maintain
enough distance to act. You remove the boulder.

Compassion = empathy + action.

## Compassionate Candor vs. Ruinous Empathy

| Mode                 | Emotional state        | Action                                 |
|----------------------|------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Ruinous Empathy      | Feeling their hurt     | Silence — withhold truth to spare them |
| Compassionate Candor | Recognizing their hurt | Speak up — remove the boulder          |

Unregulated empathy leads to burnout or withdrawal. Compassionate
Candor requires stepping past the discomfort of empathy to be direct.
