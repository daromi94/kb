# Bright idea

A flash of insight that unexpectedly reveals the solution or the
critical missing link. It cannot be forced or scheduled — it is the
byproduct of prior deliberate effort.

## The sequence

**Saturation.** Deeply investigate the problem first. The data, the
unknown, and the conditions must be fully loaded into working memory
through deliberate, often frustrating, conscious struggle.

**Incubation.** When active progress halts, step away. The
subconscious continues processing and combining elements free from
rigid, top-down constraints.

**Flash.** The idea strikes during rest or unrelated activity,
bridging the gap between the knowns and the unknown.

A bright idea is only a hypothesis. It brings a feeling of
certainty, but it must be verified rigorously — subject it to the
carrying-out phase before trusting it.

## Application questions

- Has the problem been defined clearly enough that its constraints
  can be recalled from memory?
- Has deliberate effort reached diminishing returns, signaling it
  is time to step away?
- When insight strikes, what is the smallest test that can prove
  or disprove it?
