# Decomposition and recombination

Break a complex problem into its constituent parts, examine them in
isolation, then reassemble in a new arrangement that reveals the
path between the data and the unknown.

## How it works

A problem viewed as a single entity hides its internal
relationships. Decomposing shifts focus from the overwhelming whole
to manageable details. Once isolated, details can be manipulated,
analyzed, and recombined in a novel arrangement — one that often
exposes the connection you could not see before.

## Application questions

- Can the condition be separated? Write down its parts
  individually.
- Can the data be broken down? What does one piece of information
  imply on its own?
- Can the elements be rearranged? Once broken apart, can they be
  recombined in a different order or grouping to form a familiar
  pattern?
