# Examine your guess

Treat a provisional answer as an experimental data point, not a
conclusion. The goal of a guess is rarely to be correct — it is to
observe *how* it fails.

## How it works

Plug the guess into the problem's conditions. The resulting error
reveals the direction and magnitude of the necessary correction. If
the guess is too large, the upper bound of the unknown narrows. Each
failure dictates the formulation of the next guess. This turns blind
trial-and-error into guided iteration.

## Application questions

- Does this guess satisfy the condition entirely?
- If it fails, in what exact direction does it fail?
- If the condition has multiple parts, does the guess satisfy at
  least one of them?
