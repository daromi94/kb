# Generalization

Move from a specific problem to a more comprehensive one that
contains the original as a special case.

## The inventor's paradox

A more ambitious, generalized plan may have a higher chance of
success than a restricted, specific one. Specific details and rigid
conditions obscure the underlying structure. Stripping them away
exposes the fundamental relationships. Once the general solution is
found, the specific problem is solved by reapplying the original
constraints.

## Application questions

- Can a fixed constraint be replaced by a parameter?
- Is this specific case a subset of a broader category?
- Does solving the general case reveal a pattern that makes the
  specific case trivial?
