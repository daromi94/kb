# Indirect proof

Establish the truth of a proposition by demonstrating that its
falsity is impossible. Also known as reductio ad absurdum.

## The mechanism

1. **Assume the opposite.** If the goal is to show A is true,
   begin by assuming A is false.
2. **Deduce.** Follow the logical consequences of that assumption
   rigorously.
3. **Reach a contradiction.** Continue until the chain collides
   with a known fact, a fundamental rule, or the given data. Since valid
   deduction from a true premise cannot produce a false conclusion,
   the assumption must be wrong — the original statement holds.

## Application questions

- Is the desired conclusion a negative statement? Negatives are
  hard to show directly but natural candidates for indirect proof.
- Does the direct path require checking an unbounded number of
  cases?
- If the opposite of the goal is assumed, does an obvious line of
  deduction present itself?
