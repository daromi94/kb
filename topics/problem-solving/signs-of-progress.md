# Signs of progress

Indicators that a plan is moving toward a solution rather than a
dead end. Recognizing these signals tells you when to persist and
when to abandon a failing strategy.

## Positive signs

**Data mobilization.** A previously isolated piece of given data
finds a necessary use. If a constraint that seemed irrelevant
suddenly integrates into the logic, the solution is likely
approaching.

**Simplification.** The problem's representation becomes tighter
and easier to hold in working memory. Complexity drops rather than
grows.

**Familiarity.** The transformed state of the problem becomes
structurally identical to something previously solved. Unknown
territory maps back to known territory.

## Negative signs

If the problem becomes more convoluted, requires increasingly
arbitrary assumptions, or each step feels forced rather than natural,
the current plan is failing. Complexity should decrease as you
approach the solution, not increase. Stop, step back, and devise a
new plan.

## Application questions

- Are all given conditions and data points actively involved in
  the attempted solution?
- Is the current state simpler than it was three steps ago?
- Does the path forward feel forced and irregular, or is there a
  natural flow to the next step?
