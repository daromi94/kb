# Transparent deduction

Transparent deduction means abandoning polished summaries to
construct a logical argument from first principles, step-by-step,
under active scrutiny. Formatted summaries act as black boxes — they
hide logical leaps, unverified assumptions, and errors. Forcing the
explanation into a sequential deduction strips away the momentum of
a rehearsed narrative. Every step must support its own weight. If
one fails, the progression halts immediately, allowing correction
before the final conclusion is reached.

## Derivation procedure

A derivation is a chain from given data to the unknown where each
link independently supports the structure.

**Anchor the starting point.** Isolate and state the given data.
List the exact conditions and boundaries. Exclude the desired
conclusion from the starting premises to avoid circular reasoning.

**Isolate atomic steps.** Each step relies on exactly one operation,
logical rule, or established principle. Apply one transformation
per line. State the governing rule alongside the result.

**Verify before proceeding.** Subject each step to two checks: is
it intuitively evident that the step is correct, and can it be
formally justified by identifying the exact rule or prior result
that supports the move? If it cannot be justified, the step is an
assumption, not a derivation.

**Link the sequence.** The output of a verified step serves as the
direct input for the next. If a gap exists between a result and the
next action, the chain is broken. Solve an auxiliary problem or
introduce an established result to bridge it.

**Reverse-verify.** After reaching the conclusion, traverse the
argument backward. Verify that the final state logically dictates
the preceding step, continuing until reaching the initial data.

## Application questions

When evaluating a proposed solution, demand answers to these:

**Can you defend the current step independently?** Justify the
immediate logical move based strictly on data established in
previous steps, without using the desired conclusion as
justification.

**Where exactly is the assumption?** Highlight the precise moment a
known fact is replaced by an educated guess, explicitly inviting
observers to test that specific weak point.
