# Transparent deduction

Transparent deduction means abandoning polished summaries to construct
a logical argument from first principles, step-by-step, under active
scrutiny.

## Mechanism

Formatted summaries and abstract presentations act as black boxes.
They hide logical leaps, unverified assumptions, and arithmetic
errors. Forcing the explanation into a sequential deduction strips
away the momentum of a rehearsed narrative. Every individual step
must support its own weight. If a step fails, the progression halts
immediately, allowing real-time correction before the final
conclusion is reached.

## Application triggers

When evaluating a proposed solution, demand answers to these
questions:

**Can you build it from the ground up?** Start exclusively with
undisputed given data. Do not reference the desired final state.

**Is every intermediate step visible?** Do not compress complex
deductions into a single statement. Force the breakdown into the
smallest constituent parts.

**Can you defend the current step independently?** Justify the
immediate logical move based strictly on data established in
previous steps, without using the desired conclusion as
justification.

**Where exactly is the assumption?** Highlight the precise moment a
known fact is replaced by an educated guess, explicitly inviting
observers to test that specific weak point.
